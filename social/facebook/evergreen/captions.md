## 05 — Prepare your device · `TechBros_FB_05_prepare_device.png`

Dropping off a device for repair? Do these four things first and your visit goes faster: ✅

1. Back it up — iCloud, Time Machine, or another device.
2. Turn off Apple Cash and remove your cards from Wallet.
3. Turn off Find My (Settings → your name → Find My).
4. Know your passcode — we'll need it to test the repair. ⚠️ *(I added this step; it's standard practice but isn't on your Wayfinder page. Keep or cut.)*

Can't do them all? Do what you can and we'll help with the rest at the counter. Heads up: if Find My is still on, we may not be able to service the device — it's how Apple protects you from someone else getting your phone repaired without your knowledge.

#TechBros #Ocala #OcalaFL #iPhoneRepair #TechTips

## 06 — Water resistance · `TechBros_FB_06_water_resistance.png`

Water-resistant isn't waterproof. 💧

Your iPhone's seals wear down with normal use — and they don't come back after a repair or a hard drop. Liquid damage isn't covered by any warranty, ours or Apple's.

A few things that help:
• Splash and rain are fine. Swimming, soap, and salt water aren't.
• Got it wet? Tap it gently connector-down and let it air-dry. Skip the rice and the hair dryer. ⚠️ *(Apple's own guidance; not on your Wayfinder page.)*
• Acting up afterward? Bring it in sooner rather than later — corrosion spreads.
