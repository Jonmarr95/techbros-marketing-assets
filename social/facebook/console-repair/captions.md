## 02 — PS5 HDMI · `TechBros_FB_console_02_ps5.png`

PS5 turns on but your TV says "no signal"? It's almost always the HDMI port. 🔌

A yanked cable or a console that tipped over bends the pins inside the port. The good news: the console itself is usually fine. We replace the port on the board, in-house, and you're back to gaming.

We also handle PS5 overheating (full teardown, dust-out, new thermal paste and pads), disc drives that won't read, and power problems.

Bring it in — diagnosis is free. 2383 SW College Road, Ocala.
